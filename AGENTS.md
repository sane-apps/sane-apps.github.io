# saneapps.com — agent instructions

Plain English. Short and direct.

- Static hub site for SaneApps. Product cards link to each product's own site; checkout links go through `go.saneapps.com` only (single place to change a slug).
- Every live product gets a card: Mac apps in "For your Mac", free iPhone/iPad/web products in "Free on iPhone, iPad and the web". Do not leave a shipped product unlisted.
- Deploy only via this repo's established Pages flow. Verify the live page after deploy.
- No hardcoded prices without checking the product's live source of truth.
